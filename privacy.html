Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information Shorts Video Studio (“the app”, “we”) accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices you have. Shorts Video Studio is a desktop application that runs on your own computer.

In short: the app processes your videos on your computer, has no servers of its own, collects no analytics, and never sells or shares your data. When you connect YouTube, it uses Google's permissions only to upload the clips you choose and to show your channel's name and upload status.

1. Information the app processes

Your videos and media

Videos you open or link to, and the clips, transcripts, captions, thumbnails and titles the app creates, are processed and stored on your computer, in folders you control. They are not sent to us. If you paste a link to an online video, the app downloads it to your computer so it can process it.

Google user data (YouTube)

If you choose to connect a YouTube channel, you sign in on Google's own sign-in page and grant the app these permissions:

PermissionWhat the app does with it
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload
Manage your YouTube videos		Uploads the clips you explicitly choose to publish, with the title, description, tags, visibility and schedule you review and approve.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly
View your YouTube account		Reads your channel's name, handle and picture so you can see which channel is connected, and reads the status of videos the app uploaded so it can confirm each upload (for example, whether YouTube made it public or kept it private).

The app does not read your other videos, comments, subscribers, analytics, Gmail, contacts or any other Google data, and it does not request permission to.

Google sign-in gives the app an access token and a refresh token. These are stored only on your computer: in your operating system's secure credential store (such as the macOS Keychain) when available, otherwise in a private file readable only by your user account. Your Google password is never seen or stored by the app.

Other platforms

For TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn and Threads, the app does not sign in or connect to your accounts. It prepares a caption, copies it to your clipboard and opens the platform's own upload page in your web browser; you post yourself. If you record that you posted a clip, the link you enter is saved on your computer only.

AI-written titles and descriptions

Suggested titles, descriptions and hashtags are written by an AI model running locally on your computer (through Ollama) or by a built-in template. Clip text is not sent to any outside AI service by the app.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to provide the app's features that you can see and control: making clips, showing which channels are connected, and uploading and confirming the posts you choose. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.

3. Limited Use of Google user data

Shorts Video Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Google user data is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above.
  • It is not transferred to anyone, except to Google/YouTube itself as needed to upload your videos, when required by law, or with your explicit consent.
  • It is not used or transferred for advertising, sold, or used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • No human reads your Google user data. We have no access to it: it never leaves your computer except when sent directly to Google.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information. The only transfers are the ones you start: uploading a clip sends that video and its details directly from your computer to YouTube under your account.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

  • Videos, clips and project files stay on your computer until you delete them. The app includes a Trash with permanent delete, and a cache cleanup.
  • To remove the app's access to a YouTube channel, use Accounts → Disconnect in the app, which deletes that channel's saved sign-in from your computer. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its folders removes everything it stored. Videos already uploaded to YouTube remain on YouTube until you remove them there.

6. Security

Sign-in uses Google's OAuth 2.0 with PKCE. Credentials are stored in your system's secure credential store or in a file restricted to your user account. By default the app's local interface accepts connections only from your own computer.

7. YouTube

By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
